**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to build a comprehensive business operations web and mobile application named SP. The application needs to cater to six different business types: petrol pump, hotel, FL shop, transport, slag crushing unit, and aggregate stone crusher.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
- **User Roles:**
    - **Director:** Top-level access. Manages staff, views all reports, tasks, and geolocation. Controls accounting, payroll, and authorizes indents. Can remove staff and view edit history. Requires a unified dashboard with performance data for each separate business.
    - **Manager:** Mid-level access. Manages ground staff, assigns tasks, and inputs operational data (e.g., cash expenses, stock reports). Needs a dashboard tailored to their assigned business.
    - **Ground Staff:** Entry-level access. Receives tasks and enters ground-level reports. Location is tracked for attendance.
- **Core Modules:**
    - **User Management:** Role-based access control.
    - **Task Management:** Hierarchical task allocation and tracking.
    - **Geolocation & Attendance:** Automatic location tracking for ground staff during work hours.
    - **Reporting:** Forms for various operational reports, including specific ones like running hours for crushers and options for lubricant and petrol.
    - **Accounting & Inventory:** A complete system with transaction entry, inventory tracking (opening stock, dispatch), and generation of financial statements (Ledger, P&L, Balance Sheet).
    - **Dashboards:** Role-specific dashboards with analytics. The Director's dashboard must include AI-driven predictive analytics for revenue, expenses, and inventory.
- **Feature Requests:**
    - **Financial:** Currency must be Indian Rupee (INR).
    - **Data Integrity:** Ability to edit incorrect entries with a full audit trail (edit history) visible to the Director.
    - **Notifications:** Real-time in-app notifications (via WebSockets) and email alerts for key events like task assignments.
    - **Data Export:** Export reports and ledgers to PDF and Excel.
    - **UI/UX:** Use a specific logo provided by the user, add historical trend charts, and provide language options (English, Hindi, Odia).
    - **Platform:** A Progressive Web App (PWA) with offline capabilities and a future Native Android app.

**User's preferred language**: English
